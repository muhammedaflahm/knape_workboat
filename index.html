On the boat

Air on a workboat is a working system.

Engine rooms, accommodation spaces and yard buildings all need air moved, heated or kept out. When it falls short, the crew finds out first.

01

Engine room

Fans feed the engines combustion air and pull heat out so the space stays workable. Both jobs have to hold up at sea.

02

Accommodation spaces

Heating, ventilation and galley exhaust keep crew quarters livable on long hitches and cold fronts.

03

Salt air, small spaces

Equipment has to fit where it fits and stand up to a marine environment. Specified early, not swapped late.
